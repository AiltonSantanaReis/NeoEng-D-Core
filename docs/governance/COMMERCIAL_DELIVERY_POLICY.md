# Commercial delivery policy

Baseline candidate: 1.14.0

NeoEng D-Core uses a distribution-and-contract-controlled entitlement model.
Access to an official package is granted through the authorized distribution
channel and the applicable agreement. The runtime does not contact an
entitlement service and does not place licensing state, customer identity or
commercial decisions in canonical simulation state.

The absence of in-process entitlement enforcement is deliberate. It avoids
making deterministic execution dependent on availability, wall-clock time,
account state or a commercial network service. Sector adapters may be licensed
separately and remain outside the horizontal core.

The consolidated technical package does not create or infer public
redistribution permission. `NOTICE.md` and the controlling legal documents
remain authoritative for rights of use.

Commercial statements are generated from
`audit/PRODUCT_CLAIMS_LEDGER.json`. Claims marked planned, unsupported, removed
or prohibited are never emitted as current capabilities.
